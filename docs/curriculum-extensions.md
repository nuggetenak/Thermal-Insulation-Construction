# Curriculum extensions

Additions made after the employer was confirmed. `curriculum-source.md` is left
untouched as the original record; the parser reads both files.

**Why these exist.** The original outline was written before it was known where
these four would actually work. 日建工業 is a six-person building-services
insulation firm in Iwakuni: 保温・保冷・防露・断熱工事 and duct design, on
drainage and supply pipework, HVAC ducts, refrigeration and cold-storage plant,
in offices, commercial buildings, hospitals and government buildings. One job
listing puts the scope at buildings *excluding factories*.

Three gaps followed from that:

1. **Ductwork had no coverage at all.** Zero items out of 974, for roughly half
   the daily work. Rectangular duct is a different craft from pipe — board and
   blanket rather than preformed sections, pins and washers rather than tie
   wire, different corner and joint detail. Added as chapters 37 and 38.
2. **防露 was folded into 防湿.** Related but not the same emphasis. On chilled
   water and AC ducts through a humid Japanese summer, condensation control is
   the daily craft rather than a subtopic. Added as chapter 39.
3. **Chapter 01 assumed a construction site.** Much of this work happens inside
   live, occupied buildings — hospital ceiling voids, school plant rooms in the
   holidays, offices around other trades. Added as sections 01.4 to 01.7.

Chapters are appended, never renumbered, so every existing id stays valid.
