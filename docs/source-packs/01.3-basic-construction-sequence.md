# Verified source pack for section 01.3 — Basic construction sequence

Built 2026-09-03 under `docs/source-pack-protocol.md` — the first pack made that
way, after section 01.2's pack shipped two errors that all five of its items
inherited.

**Use this as your evidence base.** Do not go looking for other figures, and do

## How this pack was built, so you know what it is worth

- Clause text was read from `mlit-kikai.txt` (pypdf extraction of the MLIT
  specification at the registry URL) and from the e-Gov API XML for 建設業法.
- `scripts/primary-sweep.mjs` was run over the 建設業法 citation list **before**
  clauses were chosen. It surfaced three articles that would otherwise have been
  missed: 第24条の2, the ただし proviso in 第24条の4第2項, and 第26条の4第2項 —
  the last of which is a duty on the worker rather than the employer. Sweep
  record is at the end.
- Every condition list below is marked **COMPLETE** or **PARTIAL**.
- Qualifiers are carried across deliberately: 除く exclusions, ただし provisos,
  and the difference between しなければならない and 努める.

## Two traps in these documents

**The specification reuses clause numbers across its 編.** `1.5.3` is
一工程の施工の確認及び報告 in 第1編 一般共通事項 — and also 冷凍機, and also
連結送水管, elsewhere in the same document. A bare "clause 1.5.3" identifies
nothing. **Name 第1編 一般共通事項 whenever you cite a 1.x.x clause in this
section**, all of which come from that 編. This is the same class of trap as the
要綱's two volumes, which caught section 01.2.

**Who a duty binds is the most useful thing about it.** Most of what follows
binds the 受注者 (the contractor) or the 元請負人. Two clauses bind the reader
personally, and those are worth more to this readership than a dozen that do
not: 建設業法第26条の4第2項 and 第25条の27第3項. Say who is bound.

## A. 建設業法 — registry id `kensetsugyoho`

Statute text is outside copyright under 著作権法第13条, but paraphrase anyway.

- **第24条の2 (下請負人の意見の聴取)** — where the 元請負人 is about to fix the
  detailed programme, the working methods, or the other matters it is for the
  prime to decide, it **must hear the subcontractor's opinion in advance**.
  A duty on the prime; in practice a right your company has, and one a new
  worker would never guess exists. Flat obligation (きかなければならない).
- **第24条の4 (検査及び引渡し)** — **COMPLETE.**
  - 第1項: when the 元請負人 receives notice from a subcontractor that its work
    is complete, it must complete the inspection confirming that completion
    **within 20 days of receiving the notice, and as soon as it can within
    that**.
  - 第2項: after confirming completion by that inspection, where the
    subcontractor asks, the prime must take delivery of the work **immediately**.
    **ただし** — this does not apply where the subcontract carries a special
    term fixing delivery for a stated day on or before the twentieth day after
    the completion time set in that subcontract.
  - Note the shape: the 20 days runs from the *notice*, not from the work
    stopping, so the notice is what starts the clock.
- **第26条の4 (主任技術者及び監理技術者の職務等)** — **CORRECTED 2026-09-03; the
  COMPLETE label it carried was false.** Two limbs were dropped: 「その他の技術上
  の管理」, which makes the three named heads examples rather than a closed list,
  and 「又は監理技術者」, so the duty in 第2項 runs to a 監理技術者 as well.
  - 第1項: the 主任技術者 must faithfully carry out the technical management of
    the work — drawing up the 施工計画, programme control, quality control, and
    the technical direction and supervision of the people doing the work.
  - 第2項: **the people engaged in the work at the site must follow the guidance
    the 主任技術者 gives in that role.** A duty on the reader. On a six-person
    company the 主任技術者 may be the person standing next to them, which makes
    this concrete rather than abstract.
- **第25条の27第3項** — a person engaged in construction work must **endeavour**
  (努める) to improve the knowledge and skills needed to do the work properly.
  An endeavour duty, not a flat obligation — do not state it as a requirement.
- **第24条の3 (下請代金の支払)** — the prime pays the subcontractor within one
  month of itself being paid, and as soon as it can within that. Included for
  completeness of the neighbourhood sweep; **no item in 01.3 needs it**, so do
  not cite it to look thorough.

## B. 公共建築工事標準仕様書（機械設備工事編）令和7年版（修補版） — `mlit-kikai-shiyosho`

All from **第1編 一般共通事項**. Registry marks this `quotable: false`: describe,
never reproduce. Governs public building work; private jobs usually follow it,
and usually is not always.

### Before work starts

- **1.2.2 施工計画書** — **PARTIAL**: (1), (3) and (4) are carried here; (2) and
  (5) are summarised loosely and should be re-read if an item leans on them.
  - (1) Before starting, produce an overall 施工計画書 (総合施工計画書) covering
    the works as a whole and submit it to the 監督職員.
  - (3) Produce a 工種別施工計画書 — the quality plan, the specific plan for
    carrying out the work, and what will be confirmed at which stage of a
    一工程 — before that work starts, and submit it. **ただし**, not where the
    監督職員 has consented in advance.
  - (4) The parts of both documents that concern the quality plan need the
    監督職員's **承諾**, and so does any change to them.
- **1.2.3 施工図等** — 施工図 and the like are produced before the work and need
  the 監督職員's 承諾 (**ただし**, not where consent was given in advance);
  produced in coordination with the related works; changes are reported, dealt
  with so the work is not disrupted, and re-consented.
- **1.5.1 施工** — the work is carried out on the basis of the design documents,
  the 実施工程表, the 施工計画書 and the 施工図等. Short, and the spine of the
  whole section: the paper decides what is built.
- **1.5.2 技能士** — **COMPLETE.** A 技能士 here means a holder of 一級技能士 or
  the single-grade qualification under the 職業能力開発促進法; which trades and
  operations apply is 特記 (stated per job). At least one such person must
  **be doing the work themselves** and **give work guidance to the others** to
  raise the quality of what is built. Evidence of the qualification goes to the
  監督職員. Relevant to these readers because the partner companies around them
  hold 1級熱絶縁施工技能士 as a matter of course.

### While the work runs

- **1.5.3 一工程の施工の確認及び報告** — on completing a 一工程, or when the
  監督職員 instructs during it, confirm the work conforms to the design
  documents and report to the 監督職員 in good time. The confirmation and the
  report are made by a person the 監督職員 has **承諾**した.
- **1.5.4 施工の検査等** — inspection by the 監督職員 where the design documents
  require it or where a report was made under 1.5.3. After a pass, later parts
  built with the same materials and method may go to sampling inspection —
  **ただし** not where the 監督職員 instructs otherwise.
- **1.5.8 施工の立会い** — **CORRECTED 2026-09-03; the COMPLETE label it carried
  was false, and the direction of the duty was wrong.** The clause requires the
  contractor's side to **receive** (立会いを受ける) the 監督職員's attendance and,
  under (2), to request it in good time — it is not a duty on the 監督職員 to
  turn up. It also carries a **ただし**: where receiving that attendance is
  difficult, a separate instruction is taken instead. The cases are where: the design
  documents say so; **main equipment is being installed**; **work is being done
  that is difficult to inspect afterwards**; the 総合試運転調整 is being done; or
  the 監督職員 specifically instructs. Request attendance in good time and take
  the 監督職員's direction on when. Provide what the attendance needs.
  *Why this one matters for insulation:* the trade's whole output is work that
  becomes difficult to inspect afterwards.
- **1.2.4 工事の記録等** — **PARTIAL**: (4) is carried, the rest summarised.
  Records, site photographs and samples must be kept where: a confirmation
  required by the design documents was done; **work is being done that becomes
  concealed as the job proceeds, or that later visual inspection cannot easily
  reach**; a 一工程 was completed; or the 監督職員 instructs. Produced on request.

### Finishing

- **1.6.1 工事検査** — **COMPLETE as to (1).** The notice that the works are
  complete may be given to the 監督職員 only where **both**: everything the
  監督職員 instructed is finished, **and** all the 工事関係図書 the design
  documents require are complete. So the paperwork is a condition of being
  allowed to declare completion, not an afterthought. Inspection happens on the
  date the client notifies.
- **1.3.11 後片付け** — on completion of the works, the parts relating to that
  work are cleared up and cleaned. Already carried by `01.2.05`; cross-reference
  rather than restating.
- **1.7.1 完成時の提出図書** — what is submitted at completion is **特記による**,
  stated per job. Where the job does not state it, the default is 1.7.2 and
  1.7.3. Carry the 特記 default rather than presenting 1.7.2 and 1.7.3 as a
  fixed list: on any given job the documents actually required are whatever the
  job says.
- **1.7.2 完成図** / **1.7.3 保全に関する資料** — completion drawings showing the
  state as finished, and the handover pack: building operating instructions,
  equipment instructions, performance test records, filings, and the
  総合試運転調整 report.
- **Handover itself is not in the specification's 第1編.** Searching it for
  引渡し and 引継 returns only 部分引渡し inside the definitions at 1.1.2 and the
  arisings clause at 1.3.9. The duty that governs handover for a company in
  this employer's position is 建設業法第24条の4 above — 元請 to 下請 — and
  whatever the subcontract says. Do not present the specification as governing
  a subcontractor's handover; it does not.
- **The numbering trap again.** 1.7.1 is 完成時の提出図書 in 第1編 and
  ユニット形空気調和機 elsewhere in the same file. That is the second confirmed
  instance after 1.5.3, so treat it as the document's normal behaviour rather
  than an oddity.

## C. Correction — what the sources do NOT carry

Searched for, and **not found**, on 2026-09-03. Recorded because a negative
result is evidence too, and because the next writer will otherwise go looking
for the same thing and be tempted to fill the gap.

The specification's 第1編 carries **no clause on correcting defective installed
work.** Its only 不合格 provision is 1.4.4(3), and that is about **materials**
failing inspection and being taken off site — not about rework of something
already installed. 1.5.4 provides for the 監督職員's inspection of the work but
第1編 does not state what follows a failure. Searching 第1編 for 手直し, 是正,
補修, やり直し and 瑕疵 returns nothing else.

**建設業法第24条の7 is not a rework clause and must not be used as one.** It
covers a 特定建設業者 guiding its subcontractors so they do not breach the Act
or the listed employment and construction laws, asking for 是正 of a breach it
finds, and — 第3項, the only flat obligation in the article — reporting to the
licensing authority where the breach is not corrected. 第1項 and 第2項 are
努めるものとする, endeavour duties. That is correction of a **legal violation**,
not correction of insulation that was installed wrong. Conflating the two would
be the exact failure `reviews/ch01-factcheck.md` finding 1 records.

The contractual remedies for work that does not conform — 契約不適合責任 and
whatever the subcontract says about putting it right — sit in the contract and
in the Civil Code, neither of which is in the registry and neither of which was
read.

**So `01.3.05` Correction has almost no citable law behind it.** Write it that
way: it is a practice item about who decides that something is wrong, who
decides who fixes it, and why hiding a defect is worse than reporting one. It
should probably carry `confidence: needs-confirmation` rather than
`standard-practice`, and it should say plainly that the reader's own contract
and supervisor govern, because this pack cannot.
