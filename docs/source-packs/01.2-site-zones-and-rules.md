## A. 労働安全衛生法 — registry id `mhlw-anzen`

- **第23条** — the employer must maintain passages, floor surfaces and stairs in
  the workplaces where it puts people to work, and must take the measures needed
  for ventilation, daylight, lighting, warmth, moisture control, rest,
  evacuation and cleanliness. This is the statutory root of "housekeeping".
- **第25条** — where there is imminent danger of an accident, work is stopped
  immediately and workers withdrawn.
- **第26条** — workers must observe the matters required of them corresponding to
  the measures the employer takes under 第20条 to 第25条.
- **第29条** — the 元方事業者 must guide subcontractors and their workers so they
  do not breach the Act, must issue correction instructions where it finds a
  breach, **and the party instructed must comply**.
- **第30条第1項** — the 特定元方事業者's duties: run a 協議組織; carry out liaison
  and coordination between works; patrol the work areas; support subcontractors'
  safety education; for industries whose workplace changes job to job, produce
  the programme and the plan for laying out machinery and equipment in the work
  area; and anything else needed to prevent accidents arising from several
  companies working in one place.
- **第32条第6項・第7項** — workers must observe the matters corresponding to
  those measures, and everyone concerned must follow instructions given to
  secure that those measures are actually carried out. This is the legal reason
  a site rule from a company that does not employ you is binding on you.

## B. 労働安全衛生規則 — same registry id `mhlw-anzen`

- **第530条 (立入禁止)** — where a place carries a risk of a person falling, the
  employer must prohibit entry by anyone other than the workers engaged in the
  related work, by displaying a notice in a conspicuous place or by another
  method.
- **第537条** — where the work may drop objects onto people, provide catch nets,
  set an exclusion area, or take other measures.
- **第552条第2項第2号 / 第3項** — where handrails or intermediate rails on a
  temporary walkway (架設通路) are removed temporarily because the work requires
  it, entry by anyone not engaged in that work must be prohibited by notice or
  another method, and the rails must be put back **immediately** once the need
  has passed.
- **第564条第1項第2号** — during erection, alteration or dismantling of a
  scaffold, entry to the area by anyone not engaged in that work is prohibited
  by notice or another method.
- **第585条 (立入禁止等)** — entry by anyone other than those concerned must be
  prohibited, with a conspicuous notice (and where prohibited by another method,
  a notice saying the place is a no-entry place), for seven kinds of place,
  among them: places handling large quantities of high-temperature objects or
  that are severely hot; large quantities of low-temperature objects or severely
  cold; harmful light or ultrasound; carbon dioxide over 1.5 percent, oxygen
  under 18 percent, or hydrogen sulfide over 10 ppm; places giving off harmful
  gas, vapor or dust; places where harmful substances are handled; places with a
  marked risk of pathogen contamination. **第2項 puts a duty on the worker**:
  people working in the same place must not enter such a place without proper
  reason.
- **第540条 (通路)** — the employer must provide safe passages to and inside the
  workplace and **keep them effective at all times**; the main ones must carry a
  marking showing they are passages.
- **第541条 (通路の照明)** — passages must have daylight or lighting to the
  degree that normal passage is not hindered.
- **第542条 (屋内に設ける通路)** — an indoor passage must have a width matched to
  its use; the surface must be kept free of tripping, slipping and
  punch-through hazards; **nothing may be placed within 1.8 m above the passage
  surface**.
- **第543条 (機械間等の通路)** — a passage between machines, or between a machine
  and other equipment, must be at least 80 cm wide.
- **第544条 (作業場の床面)** — the floor of a work area must be free of tripping
  and slipping hazards and kept in a safe state.
- **第604条 (照度)** — minimum illuminance at the working surface where workers
  are regularly employed: 300 lx for precise work, 150 lx for ordinary work,
  70 lx for rough work. *Scope caution: this is about a working surface for
  regular work, with stated exceptions. Do not turn it into a rule about head
  torches in a ceiling void.*
- **第619条** — daily cleaning, plus a thorough clean carried out at least once
  every six months, and a six-monthly survey of pests. Note that the article
  **presupposes** daily cleaning and adds the six-monthly items on top of it, so
  it is not authority for the idea that no daily duty exists. *Scope caution:
  the six-monthly deep clean and pest survey are aimed at premises an employer
  holds over time; they do not sensibly attach to a two-day job in a borrowed
  building.*
- **第536条 (高所からの物体投下による危険の防止)** — ADDED 2026-09-03 by the
  neighbour sweep, after the fact-check. Where anything is dropped from 3 m or
  more, provide a chute and post a watcher or take equivalent measures. **第2項
  binds the worker**: do not drop an object from 3 m or more where those
  measures are absent.
- **第549条 (避難用の出入口等の表示等)** — ADDED 2026-09-03 by the neighbour
  sweep. Escape exits, passages and equipment not in everyday use must be marked
  as such and kept in a state where they can be used easily. This is the clause
  behind "do not block the escape route".
- **第620条 (労働者の清潔保持義務)** — CORRECTION, added 2026-09-03 after the
  fact-check. A duty on the **worker**: attend to the cleanliness of the work
  area, and do not discard waste anywhere other than the place set aside for it.
  This was missing from the first version of this pack, and its absence led
  01.2.05 to state positively that the daily standard was trade practice rather
  than law. It is the legal root of daily housekeeping and of waste discipline,
  and it binds the reader personally rather than binding the employer.
- **第635条 / 第636条 / 第637条** — the 特定元方事業者 sets up a 協議組織 that all
  subcontractors join and that meets regularly; carries out liaison and
  coordination as required; and **patrols the work area at least once every
  working day**. 第637条第2項: a subcontractor must not refuse, obstruct or evade
  that patrol.
- **第642条の3** — on construction work, the 特定元方事業者 must give the
  subcontractor a place and the materials to brief a worker newly starting at
  that location on the state of that location, including the places where danger
  may arise, and on how the different works relate to each other — unless it
  briefs those workers itself.
- **第653条 / 第654条** — the party ordering the work must fence, rail or cover
  openings 2 m or more up where there is a fall risk, provide a means of getting
  up and down where a work platform is more than 1.5 m, and make any temporary
  walkway it lets a contractor's workers use conform to 第552条.

## C. 公共建築工事標準仕様書（機械設備工事編）令和7年版（修補版） — `mlit-kikai-shiyosho`

Downloaded in this session from the registry URL and text-searched. Clause
numbers verified in the 修補版 (令和7年11月5日 国営設第110号).

- **1.3.3 (1)** — work days and hours: no work on the statutory administrative
  holidays unless the design documents say otherwise or the 監督職員 consents in
  advance; where the documents fix the working hours, changing them needs prior
  consent; where they do not and night work is proposed, that needs prior
  consent too.
- **1.3.5 (4)** — the method of work must be set so that existing structures and
  existing piping at and around the work location are not affected; where that
  cannot be done, consult the 監督職員.
- **1.3.5 (5)** — where fire is used, or the work throws sparks, take care over
  the handling of fire and the scattering of sparks, and provide appropriate
  fire extinguishing equipment, flame-resistant sheeting and similar.
- **1.3.5 (6)** — where a third party asks for an explanation or makes a
  complaint, respond immediately and in good faith; report the dealings to the
  監督職員.
- **1.3.8 (1)** — comply with the listed environmental statutes — 建設リサイクル法
  and 廃棄物処理法 among them — and work so that noise, vibration, dust, odour,
  air pollution and water pollution do not affect the surroundings.
- **1.3.8 (2)** — keep the safety data sheet for paints, sealants, adhesives and
  other chemical products to hand, and **display the information about the
  chemicals being handled in the work area**, by labels or similar.
- **1.3.8 (3)** — during the works, work at improving the working environment
  and at keeping the site clean and orderly.
- **1.3.9 (1)(2)** — arisings: reduce, reuse and recycle them; anything to be
  handed back to the client is stored where the 監督職員 directs and recorded;
  anything to be recycled is **segregated** and taken to the designated
  facility, and that is recorded; everything else goes off site and is disposed
  of under the listed laws, and reported.
- **1.3.10 養生** — existing parts of the building and completed parts of the
  works are protected so they are not soiled or damaged.
- **1.3.11 後片付け** — on completion, the part of the works concerned is cleared
  up and cleaned.
- **1.4.3 機材の搬入** — report every delivery of plant and materials to the
  監督職員 unless prior consent removes the need.
- **1.4.4 機材の検査等** — material delivered to site is inspected by the 監督職員
  by type; once a type has passed, later deliveries of that type may go to
  sampling inspection; **material that fails is taken off site immediately**.
- **1.4.6 機材の保管** — material delivered is stored so that it is not damaged
  or degraded before it is used, and material the 監督職員 judges unfit through
  damage or degradation is taken off site immediately.
- **1.7.4 (1)** — signs required by 消防法 and similar — dangerous-goods notice
  boards, no-entry notices at plant room entrances, 火気厳禁 notices — are
  installed as part of the works. *Note the nuance: these are permanent signs
  belonging to the finished building, not temporary site restrictions. A plant
  room door can carry a no-entry sign aimed at the building's occupants while
  being exactly where you are sent to work.*

**Scope caveat, same as `01.1.03` carries:** this specification governs public
building work. Private jobs usually follow it. Usually is not always, and the
specification for the job in hand is the one that applies.

## D. 建設工事公衆災害防止対策要綱（建築工事等編） — `mlit-koshu-saigai` (new)

MLIT 告示 496号, 2 September 2019. Downloaded and read in this session. The file
holds two volumes — 土木工事編 and 建築工事等編. **Everything below is from the
建築工事等編.** The civil volume has clauses the building volume does not (its
第15 作業場の区分 is the clearest case); do not cite one for the other.

- **第1 目的** — the document exists to prevent 公衆災害, defined as harm to the
  life, body and property of third parties who are not involved in the work
  **and nuisance to them**. Nuisance is inside the definition, not outside it.
- **第2 適用対象** — it applies to building work, repair, alteration (模様替) and
  removal. Renovation inside an occupied building is squarely in scope.
- **第3** — client and contractor must comply with the listed statutes
  (建築基準法, 労働安全衛生法, 消防法, 廃棄物処理法, 建設リサイクル法 and others)
  **plus** this outline.
- **第12 (3)** — use the 新規入場者教育 to brief the people on the job about the
  parts of this outline that apply to it.
- **第14 整理・整頓** — keep the inside and the outside of the work area tidy at
  all times, taking care that dust and the like does not become a nuisance to
  the surroundings.
- **第16 粉塵対策** — where the work may raise dust, damp the source down or
  cover it.
- **第17 適正な照明** — where existing lighting is temporarily removed or moved,
  provide appropriate lighting so that people passing are not impeded.
- **第18 火災防止** — where fire is used and the law requires it, notify the fire
  station in advance. Then: keep the use of fire to the minimum directly needed
  for the work; for any other purpose designate a place with no fire risk and
  use it nowhere else; keep extinguishers and firefighting equipment matched to
  the scale of the work; do not use fire near easily ignited material; and where
  welding, cutting or similar may throw sparks, post a watcher as necessary and
  take measures to limit how far the sparks can travel.
- **第19 危険物貯蔵** — dangerous goods are stored as the law requires. Flammable
  paints, oils, other flammable materials and gas cylinders go in a dangerous
  goods store out of direct sunlight and well ventilated, marked 危険物 and
  火気厳禁, with an appointed person supervising them.
- **第21 仮囲い、出入口** — a hoarding at least 1.8 m high (3 m where
  particularly necessary) around the work area, strong enough not to blow over
  and durable for the length of the job; gates placed where they least obstruct
  traffic, **kept closed unless the work needs them open**, carrying a notice
  prohibiting entry by the public, with a traffic guard where vehicles come and
  go frequently, and hung as sliding or inward-opening doors.
- **第22 建設資材等の運搬** — survey the delivery route in advance, consult the
  relevant authorities where needed, keep noise, vibration and dust down, and
  keep track of the state of the route.
- **第30 安全巡視** — patrol the work area and its surroundings, and appoint
  someone with enough experience and knowledge of the law to do it.
- **第33 歩行者用通路の確保** — where passage has to be restricted, a pedestrian
  route separate from the carriageway must be kept: at least 0.90 m wide, or
  0.75 m where elderly people and wheelchair users are not expected, or 1.5 m
  where there are many pedestrians; effective height at least 2.1 m; separated
  from the work area by fencing or panels and from traffic by continuous
  barriers; signed, and lit at night; free of steps and unevenness, kept
  slip-resistant, with ramps, handrails and tactile paving as needed. Where that
  cannot be achieved, the contractor consults the client about changing the plan.
  **Scope caution, and this one matters:** 第33 is in the traffic chapter and is
  written about restricting roads and public footways — the pavement outside the
  hospital, not the corridor inside it. It is the figure Japanese practice
  reaches for when a job narrows a route the public uses, and that is how to
  present it. It is not a regulation about internal corridors.

## E. 廃棄物の処理及び清掃に関する法律 and its 施行規則 — `haikibutsu-shoriho` (new)

Read through the e-Gov API in this session.

- **第3条第1項** — a business must properly dispose of the waste arising from its
  own activity, at its own responsibility.
- **第21条の3第1項** — **the exception that governs construction.** Where
  construction work is carried out through successive subcontracts, the
  元請業者 — the contractor that took the job directly from the client — is the
  事業者 for the waste arising from that work. The disposal duty, the contracts
  with licensed carriers and the manifest paperwork are the prime contractor's.
- **第21条の3第2項** — the subcontractor is nevertheless **also** deemed the
  事業者 for **storage**, so the storage standards under 第12条第2項 apply to how
  it holds waste on site.
- **第21条の3第3項 with 施行規則第18条の2** — a subcontractor may carry
  construction waste itself only where the **written subcontract provides for
  it**, and only where every one of these holds:
  - the work is not demolition, and not new build or extension, and its contract
    value is 5,000,000 yen or less — or it is work to remedy defects in a
    building already handed over, of equivalent value at that same limit;
  - the waste is **not** 特別管理一般廃棄物 or 特別管理産業廃棄物 — CORRECTION,
    added 2026-09-03: this condition was missing from the first version of this
    pack. It matters more to this trade than any other item on the list, because
    廃石綿等 from removing asbestos-containing building material is specially
    controlled industrial waste under 施行令第2条の4;
  - each load is 1 cubic metre or less, **区分して運搬** — divided so the volume
    per trip is demonstrable. This is NOT segregation by waste type; do not
    conflate it with 分別 in spec 1.3.9;
  - split contracts are added back together for the ¥5,000,000 test unless split
    for a legitimate reason (施行規則第18条の2第2項) — also missing from the first
    version;
  - the destination is a facility (a transfer or storage place counts) in the
    same prefecture as the site or a prefecture adjacent to it, which the prime
    contractor owns or has the right to use;
  - the waste is not stored anywhere along the way.
  The prefecture is measured from **the site generating the waste**, not from
  the company's base — an easy error to make and a hard one to notice while the
  two happen to give the same answer. And because the destination must be the
  prime contractor's facility, carrying offcuts back to the subcontractor's own
  yard is generally outside this route entirely.
  Note also 第21条の3第4項: a subcontractor that entrusts carriage to somebody
  else picks up the 委託基準 and the manifest.
  The ¥5,000,000 attaches to the prime contract, not the subcontract. Note also 第2項 above: it is the
  contract term and the conditions that matter, not whether the offcuts fit in
  the van.
- **第12条第1項・第2項** — the 事業者 follows the disposal standards when it
  transports or disposes of industrial waste, and the storage standards while
  the waste is waiting to be transported. The detail of those standards is set
  by cabinet order and ministerial ordinance, and is the prime contractor's
  arrangement on a site — this pack does not carry that detail, so do not state
  it.

## F. Material and physics, from what is already written

- `02.3.01` and `wiley-wool-moisture` carry the wet-insulation figures: glass
  wool measured at a thermal conductivity of 0.0343 dry and 0.136 after four
  hours in a sealed chamber above 90 percent relative humidity, at 8.67 percent
  moisture by weight — and the two figures were taken by different measuring
  methods, which the paper itself flags. **If you use this at all, use it with
  that caveat, or simply cross-reference `02.3.01` instead of restating the
  numbers.** Rain on a laydown area is not the condition that was measured.

## G. The crew this is written for (from `HANDOFF.md`)

Six-person company, Iwakuni, Yamaguchi. Van-based, day trips across Yamaguchi
and western Hiroshima, projects averaging about two months. Occupied hospitals,
schools, offices and government buildings as often as new build. Pipework and
ductwork roughly an even split. On this kind of job "site zone" often means a
corridor in a working hospital, a plant room reached through a ward, or a school

## H. Things nobody has established — say so, do not fill them in

These are genuine gaps. Where an item needs one of them, write that it is not
settled and name who to ask. Do not invent a procedure.

- Whether a given site issues a written hot-work permit before cutting or
  soldering, and what it looks like.
- Hospital infection-control zoning — who sets it, what the barriers are, what
  the crew is told. Real, site-specific, and owned by `01.4.02`.
- Whether the crew may use a building's passenger lift, or a goods lift, or must
  carry material up the stairs; and how a lift car gets protected.
- Where material may be left overnight in an occupied building, and whether
  anything lockable is provided.
- Where the van may park, and what the loading window is in a hospital.
- Whether offcuts go back to the yard in the van or into the prime contractor's
  skip — legally constrained as in E above, but the actual practice at this
  employer is unknown.
- Toilets, break areas and smoking on a building the crew does not own.
- Which parts of a school are out of bounds during term time.
